Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information TSK – The Spice Kitchen, Kokapet (“TSK”, “we”, “us”) collects when you visit our website tskthespicekitchenkokapet.in or chat with us on WhatsApp, including through our automated WhatsApp assistant, and how we use it.

In short: we use your details to answer your messages, take your orders and bookings, and keep you updated about them. Optional analytics helps us understand website visits only if you accept it. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we are

TSK – The Spice Kitchen, Ground Floor, Shop No. 9 & 10, Raichandani Business Bay, Gandipet Main Road, Kokapet, Hyderabad, Telangana 500075, India. Phone +91 99490 51777, WhatsApp +91 93986 93051.

Information we collect on WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, we receive and store:

  • your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
  • the messages you send us and the options you choose in our menus;
  • order details: items, quantities, prices, total, delivery, takeaway or dine-in, and the order’s status;
  • the delivery address or other details you choose to share in the chat;
  • the date and time of your messages and orders.

We collect only what you send us. We do not ask for card numbers, bank details or passwords on WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • to reply to your questions, show you our menu, timings and location;
  • to take, prepare and deliver your orders, and to handle table bookings and catering requests;
  • to send you updates about your order, for example when it is being prepared, ready for pickup or out for delivery;
  • to let our staff take over the chat when you ask to talk to a person;
  • to keep basic records of orders for our accounts and to resolve any complaints.

We only message you about your own orders and requests. If you would rather not receive order updates, just tell us in the chat.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. It is handled only by:

  • TSK staff who need it to serve your order or request;
  • WhatsApp and Meta, who carry the messages between you and us through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Their use of data is covered by WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy;
  • our technology providers, who host the system that runs our WhatsApp assistant and stores order records on our behalf;
  • delivery partners, only the name, phone number and address needed to deliver your order, if we use one;
  • authorities, if we are required to by law.

Our website

The website has order and booking fields. Your order name and delivery address are saved in this browser as tsk.who, alongside your cart, order type, menu category and cached menu. These help you continue an order and are separate from optional analytics. Other people using the same browser may see remembered details. You can remove them using your browser's site-data controls.

Booking name, optional phone and notes are used on this page to prepare a WhatsApp link; the booking form does not explicitly save those fields in browser storage. When you open an order or booking link, the prepared message, including the details you entered, is passed to WhatsApp in the link's URL. Review it there and press Send to send it to TSK. WhatsApp may receive the link details as soon as you open it. Do not enter payment credentials or information you do not want to share.

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, menu data from Google Sheets, photos from Google Drive, a Google Maps embed, and animation libraries from Cloudflare's cdnjs and jsDelivr. These providers may receive your IP address and browser/request details when their content loads, independently of your analytics choice. External buttons connect to the named service when opened.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is off unless you choose Accept analytics. Rejecting it does not affect the menu, ordering or booking. After acceptance, Google receives basic visit information, such as a public page path, a fixed page title, time of visit, browser/device information and an online identifier in analytics cookies. Google receives your IP address in the network connection. We use these measurements to understand visits and improve the website, not for advertising or ad personalisation.

We exclude URL query strings, fragments, referrers, WhatsApp message links, order/booking fields and contact or delivery details from our analytics parameters. We do not use automatic outbound-link, form or site-search tracking. We do not enable Google Signals or user-provided data collection.

Your analytics choice is stored on this device. Use Analytics settings on either page at any time to reject or withdraw. Withdrawal disables future collection on this page, removes applicable first-party Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to unload the analytics code. It does not erase measurements already received by Google. Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 90 days without renewal; your choice remains until you change it or clear browser site data. Private browsing or storage restrictions can prevent your choice being remembered.

Google's privacy policy explains Google's processing. Contact us using the details below for questions about data already collected.
